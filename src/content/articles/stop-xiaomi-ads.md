## Step 1: Kill the "MSA" Core (The Most Important Step)

MSA stands for **MIUI System Ads**. It is the hidden background engine responsible for pushing ads into all your system apps. If you kill MSA, you kill 80% of the ads instantly.

1. Open your phone's **Settings**.
2. Scroll down and tap **Passwords & security**.
3. Tap on **Authorization & revocation**.
4. Scroll down until you see **msa** in the list.
5. Toggle it **OFF**.

> **Pro Tip:** Xiaomi really doesn't want you to do this. When you toggle it off, a 10-second countdown will appear. You must wait for it to hit zero before the "Revoke" button turns blue. *If it says "Couldn't revoke authorization", just try it again 2 or 3 times. It will eventually work.*

## Step 2: Stop the "Security Scan" Ads

When you download a safe app from the Google Play Store, Xiaomi intercepts it with a fake scan screen just to show you an ad. Let's turn that off.

1. Open the **Security app** (the green shield icon on your home screen).
2. Tap the **Settings gear icon** in the top right corner.
3. Scroll all the way to the bottom.
4. Toggle OFF **Receive recommendations**.

## Step 3: Clean up the File Manager

1. Open the **File Manager**.
2. Tap the hamburger menu (three horizontal lines) in the top left corner.
3. Tap **Settings**, then tap **About**.
4. Toggle OFF **Recommendations**.

## Step 4: Stop Xiaomi from Tracking You

Even with ads disabled, Xiaomi by default tracks your app usage to build a profile on you. 

1. Go back to main **Settings**.
2. Tap **Passwords & security**, then tap **Privacy**.
3. Scroll down to **Ad services**.
4. Toggle OFF **Personalised ad recommendations**.
